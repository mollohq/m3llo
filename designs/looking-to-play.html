Toast. A small card in the top right corner. It does not dim or block the window and does not take the keyboard. It shows once for a check, with the check sound, and closes after 12 seconds, on its close button or on an answer. It names the crew, because the check can come from a crew you do not have open. It does not show when the card is on screen already. A member can ask once in 10 minutes, so nobody can fire it again and again.
